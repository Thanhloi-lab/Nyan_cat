Generating a Perfect Looping MP4 for Case Screens

Because web browsers cannot render highly optimized hardware-compatible H.264 MP4 videos directly due to licensing limits, this generator outputs a lossless WebM video. Follow these simple steps to convert it into a perfectly looped MP4 suitable for Wallpaper Engine, Aida64, or any media player:

1
Download your video: Use the "Record WebM Video" button in the Export tab to download `nyan_cat_1920x462.webm`.
2
Run the built-in Batch Script: Copy your downloaded `nyan_cat_1920x462.webm` into this project directory, and simply double-click convert_mp4.bat. It will compile your WebM to MP4 instantly.
3
Manual Terminal Command: If you have FFmpeg installed on your path, open PowerShell in the download folder and run: 
ffmpeg -i nyan_cat_1920x462.webm -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -b:v 4M nyan_cat_1920x462.mp4
